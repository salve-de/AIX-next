# AIX Next — Product Strategy

## Mission

AIX Next helps a B2B company understand and improve the moment before a buyer contacts sales: the moment the buyer asks an AI which vendors should be considered.

The product answers four questions:

1. In which buying questions does the company enter the AI shortlist?
2. Which competitors enter instead?
3. Which cited facts or third-party sources support that outcome?
4. What verifiable evidence should the company add or clarify next?

## Problem

A company can have a sound product and a functional website while still being absent from AI-generated vendor shortlists. Existing visibility tools often require users to define prompts and competitors in advance, or stop at a share-of-voice dashboard. That creates three gaps:

- the company does not know the relevant AI buying market;
- the metric is difficult to audit;
- the dashboard does not convert missing information into an executable task.

## Initial customer

Japanese, high-value, non-regulated B2B companies where one qualified lead has meaningful value and buyers compare vendors before contacting sales.

Priority segments:

- B2B SaaS;
- IT and implementation services;
- HR, finance operations, sales and back-office software;
- professional and consulting services;
- niche industrial B2B products with public product information.

Excluded at launch:

- medical diagnosis or treatment;
- investment or financial-product recommendations;
- legal advice;
- local restaurant/hotel discovery;
- frequently changing retail inventory;
- products where unsafe recommendations create material harm.

## Core job

> When buyers use AI to compare vendors before contacting sales, show me the questions where my company falls out of the shortlist, the competitor and evidence that replace it, and the smallest fact or change needed to improve the decision surface.

## Product promise

AIX Next provides an explicit, repeatable observation panel. It does not promise a universal ChatGPT rank, guaranteed citation, or causal revenue uplift.

## Differentiation

### Zero-setup market discovery

The user starts with one company URL. AIX Next derives the operating company, brand, product category, buyer, use cases, likely substitutes and Buyer Prompt panel.

### Buyer-loss model

The primary unit is not a vague visibility score. It is a buying question with a recorded outcome:

- company recommended;
- competitor recommended;
- company omitted;
- citations used;
- stability across repeated runs.

### Evidence operating system

AIX Next converts missing public information into a specific request:

> Average implementation time could not be verified. This evidence gap is related to 11 tracked buying questions.

The user is asked only for facts the system cannot find.

### Auditability

Every metric can be traced to the exact prompt, provider, time, response, citation, extraction version and denominator.

### Longitudinal watch

The product is not a one-time PDF. A fixed Core panel is rerun weekly. Discovery prompts are kept separate so changing the questions cannot masquerade as improvement.

## Business model

### Free Scan

- no account or card;
- one URL;
- 12 Buyer Prompts;
- OpenAI, Gemini and Perplexity;
- one run per prompt and surface;
- visible result, citations, top evidence gaps and first action.

### 14-day Watch

- account/email after the result;
- fixed 30-prompt panel;
- baseline and rerun;
- evidence inbox;
- no automatic paid conversion.

### Founder Watch

- ¥29,800/month before tax;
- one brand;
- 50 fixed Core prompts plus 20 Discovery prompts;
- three AI surfaces and three repetitions;
- weekly measurement;
- full answers, citations, evidence tasks, actions and history.

## North-star metric

**Paid Project Activation:** within 14 days of payment, the project completes a valid weekly Core scan and the customer completes at least one evidence task.

This metric tests whether AIX Next both operates and changes customer behavior. Recommendation Coverage alone is too dependent on external model changes.

## Long-term moat

The durable asset is not the interface. It is the structured history:

```text
Company × Market × Buyer Intent × AI Surface × Competitor
× Citation × Evidence State × Action × Before/After × Confidence
```

With sufficient observations, AIX Next can rank actions by outcomes observed in comparable companies rather than generic SEO advice.

## Product boundaries

AIX Next will not:

- claim to control external AI systems;
- sell paid ranking placement as independent measurement;
- generate fake reviews or forum posts;
- publish unsupported “No.1” or ROI claims;
- make direct production changes without explicit approval and rollback;
- treat an API observation as every consumer AI experience;
- infer causation from a simple before/after change.
