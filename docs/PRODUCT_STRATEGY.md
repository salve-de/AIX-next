# AIX Next Product Strategy

## 1. Mission

AIX Next makes the pre-sales AI buying journey observable.

A company may rank in traditional search and still be absent when a buyer asks an AI system:

- which vendor is best for a specific company size
- which product is easier to implement
- which alternative is safer or more credible
- which provider has evidence for a particular use case

AIX turns those questions into a repeatable measurement panel and connects the outcome to public Evidence.

## 2. Problem hierarchy

### Surface problem

“We do not know whether ChatGPT recommends us.”

### Commercial problem

Prospects may form a vendor shortlist before visiting the company site or speaking with sales.

### Operational problem

Even when a company sees a visibility score, it still does not know:

1. which buying questions are lost
2. which competitor wins instead
3. which source supports the winner
4. which company fact is missing
5. which change should be made first
6. whether the same panel changes later

AIX is built for the operational problem, because that is where recurring willingness to pay remains after visibility checking becomes inexpensive.

## 3. Initial customer

Japan-based, high-value, non-regulated B2B companies where one qualified opportunity is worth more than a month of AIX.

Priority:

- B2B SaaS
- IT and business services
- HR, finance operations, sales operations and back-office software
- consulting and professional services
- niche B2B products with a public website and clear buying alternatives

Not initially supported:

- medical advice
- regulated financial recommendations
- legal advice
- local restaurant / map-first businesses
- inventory-sensitive ecommerce
- products where unsafe or age-restricted recommendations create disproportionate risk

## 4. Job To Be Done

> When a prospect uses AI to compare vendors before contacting sales, show me which questions exclude my company, which competitor is chosen, what evidence supports that choice, and what company information is missing, so that I can improve the information environment and track whether the same market changes.

## 5. Product definition

AIX is not:

- an article generator
- a one-time SEO audit
- a universal ChatGPT ranking
- a paid directory that sells ranking position
- an automated review or forum-spam system

AIX is:

- a dynamic AI buyer-market model
- an auditable recommendation panel
- an Evidence operating system
- a longitudinal Watch service

## 6. Core product loop

```text
Observe
→ Compare
→ Explain
→ Ask only for missing company facts
→ Prioritize
→ Watch the same market again
```

## 7. Differentiation

### Zero-configuration market creation

The user does not begin by entering prompts and competitors. AIX begins with the company URL and constructs:

- legal company and brand
- primary product or service
- category and use cases
- target buyer
- realistic substitutes
- high-intent Buyer Prompts

### Buyer-prompt losses, not a generic score

The unit of value is a buying question where the company is shortlisted or excluded.

### Evidence gap workflow

AIX asks for facts only after showing why they matter:

> Standard implementation time could not be verified. This Evidence gap is related to four tracked Buyer Prompts.

### Auditability

Every metric can be opened to the raw answer, source, prompt, provider, time and repetition.

### Longitudinal Watch

The product’s paid value is not “more rows.” It is the continued reconstruction of the same competitive market.

## 9. North Star

Paid Project Activation:

A paid Watch that completes a valid weekly Core measurement and receives at least one company Evidence answer within 14 days.

This avoids using an externally volatile AI visibility score as the product-health metric.
